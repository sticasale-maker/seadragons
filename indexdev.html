Data quality & cleaning backlog source: SeadragonSearch KML · 6,320 encounter records

This is an audit of the raw Wildbook / KML export as it arrives, before the explorer touches it — the goal is to show what would need fixing to clean the data at source. The explorer already works around most of these at load time (for example, it ignores the messy location text and assigns every sighting to a dive site by GPS), but the underlying records still carry the issues below.

fix at source — affects correctness normalise / verify handled / informational
📍

Location field (free text)

normalise
  • 174 distinct text strings describing ~22 real dive sites
  • 187 blank · 24 say only “Australia” · 6 are raw coordinates pasted in
  • 15 non-place entries — “Scuba diving”, “Walking my dogs at Forresters Beach”, “outer weedyland”, “depth 16m, under sewage treatment plant”
  • One site, many spellings: Old Man's Hat ×9, The Gap ×6, Kurnell ×5, Blue Fish Point ×4, Henry's Head ×4
  • Mixed granularity — sub-features (“Sponge Garden”, “Whale Watch Station”, east/west sides) recorded as whole locations

To clean: replace free text with a controlled picklist; reject coordinates, “Australia” and prose. Explorer already assigns sites by GPS, not this field.

🛰️

Coordinates / GPS

fix at source
  • 14 malformed coordinate strings — depth written with a double minus (“--20.0”), which breaks numeric parsing
  • 406 low-precision longitudes (≤4 decimals ≈ ±10 m or coarser)
  • Heavy stacking — single identical points hold 64, 50, 43 records, i.e. GPS snapped to a dive-site default rather than the in-water position
  • 0 missing coordinates · ~30 sightings outside the core Terrigal→Wollongong range (now mapped to Jervis Bay, Shellharbour, Swansea, Broughton Island & Kiama)

To clean: repair the 14 double-minus depths; treat clustered coordinates as site-level, not metre-level — fine-scale spatial pattern within a site isn't real.

🐉

Individual matching

verify
  • 6,277 of 6,320 records matched to a catalogued individual
  • 43 encounters are unidentified — no individual ID prefix, so the animal couldn't be photo-matched
  • 454 distinct individuals resolved

To clean: review the 36 unmatched encounters — attempt re-identification, or confirm them as genuine one-off sightings.

🗓️

Dates & time-of-day

verify
  • 0 records missing a date — range spans 1988 → 2026
  • 7 pre-2010 records (1988, 1989, 2000×2, 2008, 2009×2) — likely retro-entered historical sightings that skew the left edge of any trend
  • 1,365 records have a date but no time-of-day — so time-of-day charts cover ~78% of the data

To clean: verify the pre-2010 outliers; treat time-of-day as partial coverage, not a complete signal.

📏

Depth

normalise
  • 149 records have no depth recorded (0 in the KML altitude field) — treated as not-recorded, not the surface
  • 14 depths unreadable (the double-minus coordinates above)
  • Recorded depths range −3 m to −36 m — all plausible (depth carried in the KML coordinate altitude field)

To clean: store “not recorded” as null, not 0, so it's excluded from depth statistics. Explorer already treats 0 as not-recorded.

⚥

Sex

minor
  • Male 1,686 · Female 1,411 · Unknown 1,264 (~29%)
  • 7 records have a blank sex field rather than “unknown”

To clean: set any blanks to “unknown”. The 1,497 unknowns are legitimately undetermined and don't need fixing.

🗺️

Coverage — read with care

context
  • ~96% of records resolve to Kurnell — a diver-effort concentration, not seadragon abundance
  • Voodoo and Oak Park are valid picklist sites with 0 sightings so far
  • A provisional “Wollongong” site holds the handful of Illawarra-coast records (the picklist had no Wollongong entry)

Not a defect: counts and site/time concentrations reflect survey effort and coverage, not population size — keep this in mind when reading every chart.
